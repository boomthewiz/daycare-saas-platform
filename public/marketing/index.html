A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

A little clarity
before you begin.

Can I bring my roster from Procare?

Yes. Import basic children and staff from CSV/TSV exports, with separate guides for Procare Online, Desktop and SchoolCare Works. You can also paste spreadsheet cells. Review duplicate IDs, missing data and staff roles before confirming. This is a file import, not live Procare synchronization.

Do I need a credit card to try ReJoyce?

No. New organizations can explore ReJoyce for 30 days without a credit card. You can review the plan details during signup and in your account.

Can parents sign and respond to reports?

The signature and response framework is available for reviewed reports, with separate guardian rights. Parent access and notification delivery require manual setup and are not activated automatically. Request a setup review from your organization’s Optional features page.

Does choosing a feature give my staff access?

No. Your organization owner manages staff access separately. Optional features are reviewed before activation, so selecting an interest here does not change your team’s access.
